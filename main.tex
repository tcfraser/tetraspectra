\section{Tetrahedral sums}
{\color{blue}
    This section will include a formal statement of the problem under investigation, its relation to Horn's original problem and other generalizations of Horn's problem.
    This section will hopefully briefly describe what is known about the eigenvalues of triangular sums of matrices.
    Regarding tetrahedral spectra, we will describe many of the symmetries of the problem statement and various ``gauge'' choices that one could make.
    Additional generalizations of the problem statement, like to more matrices, linear combinations, etc. will be mentioned here.
    It will be important to mention the manner in which the analogue of the ``Thompson-Klyachko'' theorem does not hold, i.e. singular values of tetrahedral products are not the exponentials of eigenvalues of tetrahedral sums in general.
}

\section{Recoupling theory}
{\color{blue}
    The purpose of this section is widen the bridge between representation theory and Horn-type problems.
    We will begin with a treatment of the connection between triangular spectra and the Littlewood-Richardson coefficients and discuss its connection to symplectic geometry and moment maps.
    Then we will articulate the manner in which tetrahedral spectra are related to the norm of the recoupling operator for $\SU(d)$ and discuss the manner in which convexity results for moment maps cannot be applied and thus one must not expect the space of tetrahedral spectra to be convex (indeed it is not).
}

\section{Spectral embedding and quantum information theory}
{\color{blue}
    The purpose of this section will be to re-articulate the spectral embedding defined in \cite{christandl2018recoupling} and state the sense in which the tetrahedral spectra problem is an instance of the quantum marginal problem.
    From here we can discuss the extent to which known inequalities coming from quantum information theory yield constraints necessarily satisfied by tetrahedral.
}
